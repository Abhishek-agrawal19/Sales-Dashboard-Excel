📊 Sales Analysis Dashboard | Data Analytics Project

I’m excited to share my Sales Analysis Dashboard project, developed to transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights and support data-driven decision-making.

🎯 Project Objective
To analyze sales performance, identify business trends, evaluate product performance and uncover opportunities for business growth.

📌 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
•	💰 Total Sales: $2,297,201
•	📈 Total Profit: $286,397
•	📊 Profit Margin: 12%
•	📦 Total Quantity Sold: 37,873

🔍 Key Business Insights
1.	Technology contributes the highest category sales at 37%.
2.	Consumer customers account for 51% of total sales.
3.	New York City leads city sales with $256,368.
4.	Phones are the top-selling product, generating $330,007.
5.	November records the highest monthly sales at $349K.
6.	February records the lowest monthly sales at $60K.
7.	Standard Class accounts for 59% of shipping.

💡 Business Recommendations
•	Focus on high-performing products.
•	Improve sales during weaker months.
•	Optimize inventory management.
•	Develop targeted customer-segment strategies.
•	Monitor regional sales and profitability.
•	Improve operational and shipping efficiency.
